LEED v4 Retrofit Field Audit

Student Field Trip Checklist

1. Location & Transportation (LT)

Observe bike racks, EV charging stations, or nearby transit stops.

Observe walkability to nearby services, restaurants, or residential areas.

2. Sustainable Sites (SS)

Observe high-reflectance roof materials, green roofs, or shaded hardscapes.

Observe shielded exterior light fixtures pointing downward.

Observe permeable pavers, rain gardens, bioswales, or cisterns on-site.

3. Water Efficiency (WE)

Observe low-flow aerators on faucets, dual-flush toilets, or WaterSense labels.

Observe smart irrigation controls, drip systems, or native landscaping.

4. Energy & Atmosphere (EA)

Observe high-efficiency HVAC units, retrofitted LED lighting, occupancy sensors.

Observe rooftop solar PV arrays, solar water heating, or heat pumps.

5. Materials & Resources (MR)

Observe centralized multi-stream recycling stations (paper, glass, compost, e-waste).

Observe salvaged interior elements, rapidly renewable flooring, or low-impact finishes.

6. Indoor Environmental Quality (EQ)

Observe high-efficiency MERV air filters, CO2 sensors, or entryway walk-off mats.

Observe direct lines of sight to outdoor views and glare controls (blinds/shades).
