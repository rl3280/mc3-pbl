### Core Pedagogical & Policy Enhancements (Extracted from *AE Spring 2024*)

#### 1. Authentic Context over Applied Context (Stone’s NRCCTE Principle)

* **The Insight:** James R. Stone III emphasizes that CTE curricula must begin with **authentic technical requirements**, not abstract math with a thin coat of trade context.


* **Application to your 48-Hour Course:** Rather than presenting standard decimal worksheets and calling them "green," each lesson begins directly with the trade problem (e.g., *Sizing ductwork for an all-electric VRF heat pump system in a high-rise retrofit*). The math (fractions, decimals, ratios) is taught as the essential technical tool required to solve the trade problem.



#### 2. Cognitive Apprenticeship & Psychological Safety (Kirschner et al. / Rosenshine)

* **The Insight:** Rosenshine's principles and Cognitive Apprenticeship require explicit modeling by experts, followed by guided practice in a **psychologically safe environment** where novices feel secure asking questions.


* **Application to your 48-Hour Course:**
* **Instructor Modeling:** Step-by-step "Think-Alouds" for complex green calculations (e.g., heat loss, board feet, solar shading).


* **Peer-to-Peer Scaffolding (ZoPD):** Mixed-ability group work where participants collaborate on green retrofit proposals, lowering math anxiety and accelerating skill mastery.


* **Fading to Digital Artifacts:** Transitioning to student-led Padlet/Miro board presentations, allowing participants to demonstrate their math logic visually to funders and apprenticeship coordinators.



#### 3. Climate Justice & Just Transition (Vachon / Karp Framework)

* **The Insight:** Todd E. Vachon highlights the importance of preparing workers for a **"Just Transition"**—connecting industrial labor, green technology, environmental advocacy, and high-wage union careers.


* **Application to your 48-Hour Course:** Frame the green math curriculum as empowering women at NEW to become leaders in New York’s green economy—capable of both building clean energy infrastructure and calculating its direct community and environmental benefits.
