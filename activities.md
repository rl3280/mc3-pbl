---

## CREW BRIEFING / QUICK FIELD MATH REFRESHER

* **Roof Pitch / Slope:** Pitch is expressed as **Rise over Run** (e.g., $6/12$ means 6 inches of vertical rise for every 12 inches of horizontal run). Total Run is half the total building span.
* **On-Center (O.C.) Stud Count:** Formula for wall framing is $\text{Total Length (in inches)} \div \text{Spacing (16") } + 1 \text{ (Starter Stud)} + \text{Waste/Corners}$.
* **Stair Geometry:** Ideal stair rise per step is **$7" \text{ to } 7\ 1/2"$**. Total Rise = floor-to-floor height in inches. Number of Risers = Total Rise $\div$ Target Rise (rounded to nearest whole number).
