# **Expanded Strategic Messaging Matrix**

**Program:** NEW 48-Hour Integrated MC3 Green Math Curriculum (Units 5 & 8\)  
**Partners & Funders:** BlackRock, NYPA, NYSERDA, CHPE Green Economy Fund, NABTU / TradesFutures, NEW

| Funder / Stakeholder | Core Strategic Priorities (from *AE Spring 2024*) | 48-Hour Green Math Curriculum Alignment & Deliverables | High-Value Metrics & Evidence Artifacts   |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **BlackRock** | **Economic Mobility & Scalable Talent Pipelines:** Scaling pathways out of systemic poverty through high-wage, high-demand industry credentials; building wealth in underrepresented communities. | Integrates trade economics, wage awareness, material estimation, board-feet cost calculations, and waste-reduction budgeting directly into Unit 5 decimal and fraction modules. | • Payback period & material cost-estimating accuracy. • Retention readiness & transition rates into high-wage Registered Apprenticeship Programs (RAPs). |
| **New York Power Authority (NYPA)** | **Building Electrification & Grid Decarbonization:** Infrastructure modernization, building envelope decarbonization, heat pump expansion, and clean energy transition. | Uses applied thermal transfer equations (Q \= U · A · ΔT), HVAC load sizing, duct loss ratios, insulation R-value to U-factor conversions, and electrical load calculations. | • Calculated net thermal efficiency improvement estimates. • Mastery of real-world energy-audit math calculations. |
| **NYSERDA** | **Quantifiable Climate Outcomes & Equity:** Standardized clean energy credentials, climate justice, and verifiable reduction of greenhouse gas emissions. | Frames math problem-sets as "Green Decarbonization Audits." Students calculate CO2 metric ton reductions, solar array output, and payback ratios alongside standard geometry/fractions. | • Project-based metric ton CO2 offset estimates. • Alignment with state clean energy standards and credentials. |
| **Champlain Hudson Power Express (CHPE) Fund** | **Renewable Infrastructure & Energy Corridors:** Transmission line infrastructure, clean energy delivery, inclusive growth, and rural-urban community reinvestment. | Incorporates renewable transmission math: cable run lengths, conduit sizing, voltage drop decimals, transmission loss percentages, and solar/wind array sizing. | • Applied electrical & infrastructure geometry problem sets. • Demonstration of workforce readiness along clean energy corridors. |
| **NABTU / TradesFutures (MC3)** | **Apprenticeship Retention & Diversity:** Standardized 120-hour curriculum, high retention in 30+ building trades, safety compliance (OSHA), and equity for women/people of color. | Fully satisfies Unit 5 (40 hrs Math) & Unit 8 (8 hrs Green Construction) requirements through contextualized, authentic trade applications rather than isolated abstract math drills. | • 77% people of color / 20% women demographic target benchmark. • High pass rates on MC3 Unit 5 proficiency exams. |
| **Non-Traditional Employment for Women (NEW)** | **Gender Equity & Supportive Services:** Removing systemic barriers to entry/retention in union trades for women (OMNY transit cards, childcare, gear, safety). | Emphasizes inclusive, psychologically safe peer-learning (ZoPD), supportive group scaffolding, and real-world female-focused trade applications. | • 100% completion of Unit 5 & 8 green math requirements. • Digital portfolio artifacts showcasing female trade mastery. |
