# 🌿 PBL Gold Standard Unit: The NYC Urban Retrofit & "Green Math" Challenge

### **Project Overview**

* **Target Audience:** Nontraditional Employment for Women (NEW) Students / Pre-Apprenticeship Trades Trainees
* **Core Subject:** MC3 Unit 8: Green Building & GPRO Fundamentals of Building Green
* **Duration:** 1 Week (Fieldwork + Padlet Lab Synthesis)
* **Format:** Field Pairs

## 1. Key Knowledge, Understanding, and Success Skills

* **Key Knowledge (GPRO & MC3):** Applying building envelope strategies, energy efficiency, and retrofit concepts to reduce building emissions.
* **Key Understanding:** Construction trades directly impact building operational energy use, climate resilience, and urban carbon footprint reduction.
* **Success Skills:** Critical observation, trade-based quantitative reasoning (*Green Math*), digital site documentation, and collaborative trade communication.

---

## 2. Challenging Problem or Question

* **Driving Question:** *How can we, as future union construction tradeswomen, identify "dirty energy" culprits in our own NYC neighborhoods and calculate the real-world carbon reductions achieved through trade-based green retrofits?*

---

## 3. Sustained Inquiry

Students move through a structured 3-stage inquiry process:

```
[ Stage 1: Field Scouting ]  ──►  [ Stage 2: Trade Retrofit Analysis ]  ──►  [ Stage 3: Green Math Calculation ]
  • Inspect exterior envelope       • Select 3 GPRO trade retrofits            • Apply NYC grid emission factors
  • Document materials/sites        • Sketch solutions to trade locals         • Quantify annual CO₂ reduction

```

1. **Observe:** Inspect a local building (residential or commercial), or fixtures in public spaces like the MTA on your daily commute, around NEW, or your neighborhood, identifying physical signs of energy waste (e.g., single-pane windows, uninsulated brick, exterior boiler vents, outdated lighting).
2. **Investigate:** Reference GPRO training materials to determine the specific trade interventions required (Carpentry, Electrical, Plumbing/HVAC, glaziers, concrete, ...) to eliminate the waste.
3. **Quantify:** Model baseline energy use versus post-retrofit savings using standardized conversion formulas.

For example,

1. **Building Location & Type:** State the address/neighborhood and building type (e.g., 4-story brick multi-family, commercial storefront, older residential house).
2. **Visual Inspection (Spotting "Dirty Energy"):** Look for energy-wasting features:
* *Building Envelope:* Single-pane windows, drafts around doors, uninsulated brick/walls, leaky AC window units.  ──►  Add continuous exterior insulation, seal air leaks, upgrade to high-performance double/triple-pane windows.

* *Lighting/Electrical:* Old incandescent or halogen lighting left on in public corridors.  ──►   Convert all fixtures to high-efficiency LEDs and install occupancy sensors or smart building controls.

* *Mechanical/HVAC:* Outdated boilers venting heat, old air conditioning compressors, or lack of smart thermostats.  ──►  Replace fossil-fuel oil/gas boilers with high-efficiency heat pumps or energy-efficient water systems.    

---

## 4. Authenticity

* **Real-World Connection:** Reflects real site-audit responsibilities under NYC energy codes and local building performance standards (e.g., [Local Law 97](https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/ll97-greenhouse-gas-emissions-reductions.page)). CHAPTER 3 MAINTENANCE OF BUILDINGS §28-308.1 Definitions.
* Students evaluate real buildings in their communities rather than textbook scenarios.
* **Trade Context:** Directly connects mathematical calculations to trade locals, framing math as an essential tool for union apprentices and journeymen.

---

## 5. Student Voice and Choice

* **Site Selection:** Pairs select any building within their home neighborhood or commute route.
* **Focus Area:** Students choose which trade disciplines and retrofits to emphasize based on their interest (e.g., focusing on electrical/solar, mechanical/heat pumps, or envelope/insulation).
* **Presentation Style:** Pairs organize and record their video documentation, choosing how to walk through and highlight site features.

---

## 6. Reflection

* **Student Reflection:** Trainees reflect on how their perspective on everyday city infrastructure shifts once they view buildings through a green construction lens.
* **Debrief Discussion:** Class debrief on how tradeswomen serve as frontline implementers of climate solutions in New York City.

---

## 7. Critique and Revision

* **Peer Feedback:** Students review at least two peer Padlet posts using the **Warm & Cool Peer Review Protocol**:
* *Warm Feedback:* Commend an accurate trade identification or effective site video breakdown.
* *Cool Feedback / Question:* Challenge or refine the "Green Math" (e.g., *"Did you account for lighting occupancy sensors in your electrical savings estimate?"*).


* **Math Calibration:** Pairs revise calculations on their whiteboards based on peer and instructor feedback before final submission.

---

## 8. Public Product

### **Deliverable:** A Single Padlet Post per Pair Containing Three Artifacts

#### **Artifact 1: 60–90 Second Site Inspection Video**

* **Building Overview:** Location, structure type, and current usage.
* **Visual Audit:** Clear footage highlighting specific energy-loss features (drafts, outdated fixtures, single-pane glass, old mechanical systems).

#### **Artifact 2: GPRO Retrofit Plan (Caption Text)**

Propose **three trade-specific retrofits**, citing trade jurisdictions:

* **Carpentry/Envelope:** Add continuous air barriers, seal leaks, or upgrade to double/triple-pane high-performance glazing.
* **HVAC/Plumbing:** Transition from fossil-fuel combustion boilers to electric air-source heat pumps.
* **Electrical:** Install LED fixtures, motion/occupancy sensors, and smart controls.

#### **Artifact 3: "Green Math" Whiteboard Photo**

A photo of a hand-drawn whiteboard or notepad displaying step-by-step carbon reduction calculations:

```text
===========================================================
               GREEN MATH RETROFIT ESTIMATE
===========================================================
Building Address: [Insert Neighborhood / Address]

1. BASELINE CONSUMPTION (Estimated):
   * Annual Energy Consumption: 50,000 kWh / year

2. GPRO RETROFIT SAVINGS (% Reduction):
   * Envelope Insulation (Carpentry):          -15%
   * Heat Pump Conversion (HVAC/Plumbing):     -20%
   * LED & Smart Controls (Electrical):        -10%
   --------------------------------------------------------
   TOTAL ESTIMATED EFFICIENCY GAIN:             45%

3. ANNUAL ENERGY SAVINGS:
   * 50,000 kWh  x  0.45  =  22,500 kWh / year saved

4. CARBON FOOTPRINT REDUCTION (CO₂ Emissions Avoided):
   * (NYC Grid Factor: ~0.85 lbs CO₂ per kWh)
   * 22,500 kWh  x  0.85 lbs CO₂/kWh  =  19,125 lbs CO₂ saved / year
   --------------------------------------------------------
   METRIC TON EQUIVALENT:
   * 19,125 lbs ÷ 2,000 lbs = 9.56 Metric Tons of CO₂ avoided / year
===========================================================

```
